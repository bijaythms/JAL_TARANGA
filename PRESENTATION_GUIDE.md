* **Stay on**: `Command Centre` (`#view-gis`)

  1. Click the **"Delineate Watershed"** button in the toolbar.
  2. Click on any river line (e.g., Periyar or Chaliyar basin).
  3. **Watch**: The system snaps to the stream outlet and renders the exact upstream catchment polygon and 4th-order dendritic stream reaches in $<25\text{ ms}$.

    > *"Here is our first major technical innovation: Point-and-Click Catchment Delineation. If Aluva or Chalakudy is flooding, an official simply clicks on that bridge or gauge.   2. Draw a rectangle over an agricultural or hill slope.

### STEP 6: Predictive Geotechnics — 3D Landslides & Cloudburst Physics
* **Step 6A — Western Ghats Landslides & 3D Terrain Mode**:
  * **Navigate to**: `Erosion & Landslides` (`#view-landslides`)
  * **What to Do**:
    1. Click the **"Switch to 3D Terrain"** button: Watch the map smoothly pitch into a **3D MapLibre elevation model** of the Western Ghats!
    2. Point out critical hotspots like **Chooralmala/Meppadi**. Move the **Soil Saturation Slider** from 40% up to 85%.
    3. **Watch**: The real-time Taylor Infinite Slope equation recalculates the **Factor of Safety (FS)** from green (>1.3) down to a red alert (<1.0 Critical Failure Imminent).
  * **What to Say (Script)**:
    > *"Rather than relying on vague color codes, our Landslide engine computes real geotechnical mechanics in full 3D elevation terrain. Using the Taylor Infinite Slope stability equation, we see how pore-water pressure from sustained rainfall drops the Factor of Safety below 1.0, triggering debris flow alerts before disaster strikes."*
* **Step 6B — Rainfall Runoff Simulator**:
  * **Navigate to**: `Rain Simulator` (`#view-simulator`)
  * **What to Do**: Select **Periyar Basin**. Switch storm intensity from **Moderate (25 mm/h)** to **Extreme (130 mm/h Cloudburst)**.
  * **Watch**: Runoff volume in MCM surges, inundated area in hectares expands, and the dual-wave hydrograph chart updates dynamically.
  * **What to Say (Script)**:
    > *"In the Rain Simulator, emergency managers can stress-test Kerala's basins. Under an extreme 130 mm/h cloudburst, Jal Taranga computes the accumulated runoff in Million Cubic Meters and plots dynamic hydrograph curves showing the exact time of flood peak concentration."*

## 💡 Top 5 Pro-Tips for Presenting Jal Taranga

1. **Don't Rush the Map**: When you fly to a location (e.g., Meppadi), pause for 2 seconds to let the judges absorb the satellite terrain before clicking the next button.
2. **Emphasize Zero-Crash Resilience**: Mention that if external networks or database servers fail, Jal Taranga's internal deterministic fallbacks ensure the platform never freezes in the field.
3. **Showcase the Bankfull Line in Tab 7**: Evaluators love seeing the post-intervention hydrograph wave stay safely *below* the red dashed bankfull capacity line.
4. **Highlight Privacy & Safety Rails**: Mentioning encrypted citizen emails and the airport/dam statutory geofence shield demonstrates mature software engineering.
5. **Keep Tab Order Consistent**: Follow the 8-step sequence above—it takes the audience on a logical journey from problem to map, to AI, to physics, to economics, and finally to community governance.
